**Primary question:**

> When a professional says “I understand today’s market,” what beliefs constitute that present Market Understanding?

UAIA already defines Understanding as the present, stratified, evidence-bound, confidence-aware belief-state.  
ATI-003 does **not** redefine Understanding.  
ATI-003 defines **what beliefs** make up ATI’s **Market Understanding** (Environment stratum).

# 1. Definition of Market Understanding

**Market Understanding** is ATI’s present Environment Understanding of the Trading Environment (ATI-001): an evidence-bound, confidence-aware, freshness-bearing, falsifiable belief-state over irreducible market-environment belief categories — including what is unknown, contested, and what would invalidate the state — and containing no agent-body state, no forecasts, no trade directives, and no strategies.

**Constitutional sentence:**

> “I understand today’s market” means: I presently hold a coherent, evidence-bound belief-state about the Trading Environment’s context, regime, forces, confirmation, structure, interaction conditions, field co-movement, overlays, and fragility — with confidence, unknowns, freshness, consistency, and invalidation — not that I have a price, an indicator, a story, or a trade.

---

# 2. Architectural Purpose

1. Give ATI a **living market mind** a professional would recognize.  
2. Specialize UAIA Understanding for markets without redefining UAIA.  
3. Consume Facts (from ATI-002 percepts via Acceptance/Assimilation — processes out of scope) as evidence bindings only.  
4. Provide the descriptive premise for later Decision Frame / Decision documents.  
5. Forbid smuggling of strategy, opportunity, and prediction into “understanding.”

---

# 3. Market Understanding Principles

**MU-01 Presentness** — Beliefs are about the situation now (including currently developing conditions as present dynamics).  

**MU-02 Evidence Binding** — Beliefs cite Facts or are marked thin/unsupported.  

**MU-03 Epistemic Honesty** — Confidence, unknowns, freshness, consistency, invalidation are mandatory.  

**MU-04 Essence Discipline** — Prefer beliefs about environmental conditions over indicator totems.  

**MU-05 No Ought** — No opportunity, setup, BUY/SELL, size, stops.  

**MU-06 No Forecast Identity** — Predictions are not Market Understanding content.  

**MU-07 No Agent Body** — Portfolio, orders, margin-of-self belong to Agent Situation (separate stratum; not ATI-003).  

**MU-08 Falsifiability** — Every live belief family carries invalidation conditions.  

**MU-09 Contradiction Visibility** — Contested beliefs remain visible; no silent normalization.  

**MU-10 Narrative Subordination** — Explanatory narrative never outranks evidence-bound beliefs.  

**MU-11 Timeless Categories** — Ontology stays venue-agnostic; India/NSE instances specialize data, not categories.  

**MU-12 Developing ≠ Predicting** — “Pressure is building / acceptance is failing” is present dynamics; “will close at X” is prediction.

# 5. Belief Categories (Irreducible)

For each: why fundamental · not derived from · decisions later may use (informational only; Decision not defined here) · intraday character.

### M1 Temporal Context
**Why:** Same activity means different things by session phase/expiry proximity.  
**Not:** ATI’s Attention schedule.  
**Intraday:** Advances with institutional clock; jumps on special sessions.

### M2 External Context
**Why:** Local venue is not a closed world (rates/FX/global risk/policy backdrop).  
**Not:** A news narrative.  
**Intraday:** Usually slow; discontinuous on shocks.

### M3 Event Pressure
**Why:** Scheduled/unscheduled events create non-stationary patches.  
**Not:** Identical to External Context (time-localized).  
**Intraday:** Builds → spikes → decays.

### M4 Regime
**Why:** Generative character of the environment (trend continuum, balance, shock/chaos, liquidity stress, event-driven).  
**Not:** Volatility alone; not a single indicator.  
**Intraday:** Provisional → contested → may stabilize → can break.

### M5 Directional Pressure
**Why:** Belief about what force is in control (initiative imbalance).  
**Reject “Trend” as peer:** trend is a path expression of pressure over time — **derived**, not peer.  
**Reject “Momentum” as peer:** same — derived expression / later Reasoning artifact if forecast-shaped.

### M6 Participation
**Why:** Whether moves are broad or narrow.  
**Reject “Strength/Weakness” as peer:** derived from M5+M6+M8.

### M7 Flow Footprint
**Why:** Inferred belief about aggressive vs passive flow / absorption — always uncertain.  
**Reject “Institutional Activity” as omniscient fact-belief:** hypothesis only inside M7.  
**Must never** claim private participant Intent as Understanding.

### M8 Auction Acceptance
**Why:** Acceptance/rejection of prices is real market structure.  
**Reject “Support/Resistance” as peer:** derived map of acceptance/rejection history.

### M9 Price Location
**Why:** Where price sits relative to accepted value / opening range / prior acceptance changes asymmetry.  
**Not:** A target or stop.

### M10 Tradability
**Why:** Interaction cost/capacity conditions of the Environment (crowd absorptive capacity as believed now).  
**Reject “Liquidity” essence without conditions:** Tradability is the belief; it binds to manifestations via evidence — not a mystical pool.

### M11 Volatility State
**Why:** Amplitude and expansion/compression as present condition.  
**Not:** A forecast of future vol; not an option-pricing model identity.

### M12 Cross-Section
**Why:** Leaders/laggards/factors — market as a field.  
**Relative Strength:** lives here as comparative belief.  
**Reject “Sector Rotation” as peer:** derived path of M12 over time.

### M13 Dependence
**Why:** Co-movement/contagion beliefs — diversification reality today.  
**Not:** Identical to Cross-Section levels.

### M14 Derivatives Overlay *(conditional primary)*
**Why:** When derivatives matter, positioning/hedging pressure can dominate cash narrative.  
**Absent when irrelevant** — ontology node may be inactive, not deleted from constitution.

### M15 Fragility
**Why:** Breakability / cascade / one-way / gap potential of the Environment.  
**Independent of directional call.**  
**Not:** ATI book risk (Agent Situation / Goals — out of scope).

### M16–M20 Epistemic Wrapper
Mandatory UAIA wrappers specialized as Market Understanding properties — see §§8–12.

# 8. Confidence Model

- Confidence is **degree of commitment to present beliefs**, not probability of a forecast.  
- Separable: global Market Understanding confidence vs per-category confidence.  
- High confidence with large Unknown Set is illicit (incoherence).  
- Channel degradation (via Facts) must be allowed to **lower** confidence without forcing fake certainty.  
- Decision confidence (later) ≠ Market Understanding confidence.

---

# 9. Unknown Model

Unknown Set must explicitly list:

- uncovered instruments/venues in claimed scope;  
- contested categories;  
- event windows with unresolved impact;  
- missing evidence after integrity failures;  
- inactive but relevant M14 when derivatives matter but evidence thin.

**“I do not know”** is a valid Market Understanding state (high unknowns / low confidence), not an absence of Understanding.

---

# 10. Contradiction Model

Consistency Status ∈ {Consistent, Contested, Fragmented}.

Examples of contest:

- M5 strong directional pressure vs M6 weak participation;  
- M8 rejection vs M5 continued pressure claims;  
- M10 tradability fine vs M15 fragility extreme without reconciliation.

Contested states **increase unknowns** and **lower actionable confidence** — they do not delete evidence.

---

# 11. Invalidation Model

Every live belief family carries **Invalidation Conditions**: observable/Fact-pattern thresholds that force weaken/invalidate/refine.

Invalidation is mandatory. Dogmatic Market Understanding is unconstitutional.

---

# 12. Freshness Model

- Every state has as-of / age.  
- Freshness decay is category-sensitive (Activity-linked beliefs decay faster than slow External Context).  
- Stale Understanding must raise unknowns or lower confidence — never present as fresh by default.

---

# 13. Evidence Binding

- Beliefs bind to Fact identities/scopes.  
- Claim-Facts (publications) may raise M3/M17; they do not automatically create world-state beliefs.  
- Thin/unsupported beliefs must be marked — not silently presented as hard Understanding.

---

# 14. Belief Revision

Revision operators (conceptual, Assimilation-owned process elsewhere): reinforce, weaken, create, invalidate, refine, uncertainty↑/↓, no-op, defer.

Market Understanding exposes **revision identity** so later Decision can cite “understood as of T.”

Silent mutation forbidden.

---

# 15. Market Understanding Lifecycle

```
Empty / Prior overnight state
  → Updating (continuous during sessions and as Facts arrive)
  → Stable enough for citation
  → Contested / Degraded (integrity or contradiction)
  → Invalidated families revised
  → Session-end compression into Memory traces (storage out of scope)
```

Understanding **does not end** at the bell; it persists as present belief with session-aware Temporal Context.

# 20. Final Recommendation

**Freeze ATI-003** on the ontology **M1–M20** as the constitutional meaning of Market Understanding.

> Market Understanding is ATI’s present Environment belief-state over temporal/external/event context, regime, directional pressure, participation, flow footprint, auction acceptance, price location, tradability, volatility state, cross-section, dependence, conditional derivatives overlay, and fragility — wrapped in confidence, unknowns, freshness, consistency, and invalidation — evidence-bound and falsifiable. It is what “I understand today’s market” means. It is not a forecast, not a trade, not a portfolio, and not a story.
