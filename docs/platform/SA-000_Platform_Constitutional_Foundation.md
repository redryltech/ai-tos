# 1. Definition

## What a Platform is

A **Platform** is the shared architectural layer that provides:

1. a **shared engineering environment** for product-conforming future software compositions without becoming the product;  
2. **composition** and **lifecycle** boundaries for those compositions under constitutional order;  
3. **constitutional constraints** that hold every software component to upward conformance;  
4. common engineering boundaries, provisional module laws, and separation rules so future software compositions do not redefine UAIA, ATI, DDD, or PRODUCT;  
5. preservation of PRODUCT identity and center of gravity while allowing optional agency, tenancy, and deployment choices to vary without changing what the product *is*.

**AI-TOS Platform** is that shared engineering environment, composition and lifecycle frame, and constitutional constraint system for Autonomous Decision Intelligence over Markets (PRODUCT-002), specialized under ATI, semantically owned under DDD, cognitively rooted in UAIA.

Platform is **not** intelligence.  
Platform is **not** trading meaning.  
Platform is **not** product capability packaging.  
Platform is the constitutional **shared engineering environment, composition/lifecycle frame, and constraint system** for all future software components that realize those higher layers.

## What a Platform is not

Platform is **not**:

- the product;  
- a product capability;  
- a Bounded Context;  
- Market Understanding, Agent Situation, Decision, or Mandate Authority;  
- a redefinition of UAIA primitives;  
- a Trading Operating System identity;  
- a signal factory, strategy peer, or presentation peer;  
- Implementation (code and operational artifacts remain below Platform Architecture);  
- ownership of business language or aggregate truth;  
- ownership of cognitive loops or learning activation.

If a concern defines *what the product is* or *what intelligence/business means*, it does not belong to Platform.

# 2. Platform Purpose

AI-TOS requires a Platform layer because higher constitutions define **meaning** and **capability**, but do not by themselves define how many future software compositions and provisional modules may coexist without collapsing those meanings.

**Platform exists to:**

1. Separate **product identity** (PRODUCT) from **host architecture** (Platform);  
2. Guarantee that every future software component **implements** frozen constitutions rather than redefines them;  
3. Hold **engineering laws** that all provisional modules and Platform artifacts share: separation, dependency direction, boundary integrity, and upward conformance;  
4. Allow future application compositions to realize PRODUCT capabilities within a shared engineering environment, composition, and lifecycle frame — without each composition inventing its own constitutional hierarchy;  
5. Keep optional agency and operational variation below PRODUCT so they cannot redefine Autonomous Decision Intelligence over Markets.

Without Platform, Implementation tends to absorb PRODUCT, DDD, ATI, and UAIA into ad hoc structure — which AI-TOS-000 forbids.

# 5. Relationship to UAIA

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| Authority | **UAIA always outranks Platform.** |
| Role | UAIA owns Intelligence: primitives, loops, invariants, Must-Never-Overlap for cognition. |
| Platform duty | Provide shared engineering environment, composition, lifecycle, and constitutional constraints so future software compositions **preserve** UAIA separations (Understanding primary; Learning proposes; Governance activates; no silent self-mutation of production meaning). |
| Forbidden | Platform may not invent, rename, collapse, or reinterpret UAIA primitives. |

Platform is a **consumer and enforcer** of UAIA conformance, never a peer author of intelligence.

# 6. Relationship to ATI

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|

ATI specializes UAIA. Platform provides shared engineering environment, composition, lifecycle, and constitutional constraints for ATI-conforming future software compositions. Platform does not specialize trading meaning.

# 7. Relationship to DDD

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| Authority | **DDD always outranks Platform.** |
| Role | DDD owns business semantic ownership: Bounded Contexts, ubiquitous language, aggregates, commands, events, read models, domain/application services, ACL. |

# 8. Relationship to PRODUCT

| Rule | Statement |
|---|---|
| Authority | **PRODUCT always outranks Platform.** |
| Role | PRODUCT owns product identity, mission, permanent capabilities, center of gravity, and non-identity exclusions. |
| Platform duty | Provide shared engineering environment, composition, lifecycle, and constitutional constraints so future software compositions realize PRODUCT-002 capabilities; preserve Understanding → Decision as center of gravity; keep Presentation/Signal/Strategy/Opportunity/Trading-OS out of peer capability status. |
| Forbidden | Platform may not redefine product identity, add peer capabilities, or make optional agency define the product. |

**Product Architecture vs Platform:**

- **PRODUCT** answers: *What is the product, and which capabilities are permanent?*  
- **Platform** answers: *How does the shared engineering environment, composition, lifecycle, and constitutional constraint system keep future software compositions subordinate to that product constitution?*

PRODUCT-002 already states: platform/infrastructure identity is not product identity — **AI-TOS remains platform**. SA-000 freezes that separation as Platform law.

**Platform vs future application compositions:**

- **Platform** is the shared engineering environment, composition and lifecycle frame, and constitutional constraint system.  
- **Future application compositions** are product-facing software compositions that realize one or more PRODUCT capabilities under Platform laws. Their formal constitutional definition is deferred.  
- Future application compositions **depend upward** on constitutions and **laterally** only as Platform allows.  
- Future application compositions never amend PRODUCT by structural convenience.

# 9. Platform Invariants

**PI-1 — Subordination**  
Platform is always below UAIA, ATI, DDD, and PRODUCT. Higher constitutions always win.

**PI-2 — Shared environment, not product**  
Platform’s shared engineering environment, composition, lifecycle, and constraints never become product identity. AI-TOS Platform ≠ ATI product.

**PI-3 — Meaning elsewhere**  
Cognitive meaning (UAIA/ATI), business meaning (DDD), and capability packaging (PRODUCT) are never owned by Platform.

**PI-4 — Upward conformance**  
Every Platform document and every software component under it must conform upward to AI-TOS-000 and all higher frozen layers.

**PI-5 — Center of gravity preserved**  
Platform structure must not invert PRODUCT center of gravity away from Understanding → Decision Authority.

**PI-6 — Optional agency remains optional**  
Enactment/agency variation allowed under Platform must not redefine product identity.

**PI-7 — Provisional module ≠ Constitution**  
Provisional software modules implement constitutions; they do not replace them. (Module is referenced provisionally here. Its formal constitutional definition is introduced in SA-002.)

**PI-8 — No silent amendment**  
Structural convenience in Platform or future software compositions must not silently amend frozen semantics.

**PI-9 — Breaking change discipline**  
Platform semantic breaks require **Platform v2** per AI-TOS-000. Platform must never force a higher-layer major version by unilateral redesign.

**PI-10 — Implementation is below**  
Implementation artifacts are not Platform Constitution. They obey Platform and all layers above.

**PI-11 — Technology neutrality**  
Platform remains technology-neutral.  
Platform Architecture must never depend upon or be defined by programming languages, frameworks, databases, cloud providers, middleware, messaging systems, operating systems, or implementation technologies.  
Technology choices belong below Platform Constitution.

# 10. Must Never Overlap

The following pairs **must never be collapsed** inside Platform Architecture:

| Must never overlap | Reason |
|---|---|
| **Platform ↔ Product** | Shared engineering environment is not identity. |
| **Platform ↔ Intelligence (UAIA)** | Platform does not own cognition. |
| **Platform ↔ Trading Cognition (ATI)** | Platform does not specialize markets-as-meaning. |
| **Platform ↔ Bounded Context (DDD)** | Provisional module frame ≠ semantic owner. |
| **Platform ↔ Product Capability (PRODUCT)** | Capabilities are PRODUCT-owned; Platform only provides environment, composition, lifecycle, and constraints for their realization. |
| **Platform ↔ Future application composition** | Shared environment ≠ a single application composition. |
| **Platform ↔ Implementation** | Architecture laws ≠ operational artifacts. |
| **Understanding ↔ Execution (as product center)** | Platform must not center the environment on enactment. |
| **Learning ↔ Governance activation** | Platform must not allow structural paths that make Learning self-activate production competence. |
| **Presentation / Signal / Strategy / Opportunity / Trading-OS ↔ Peer product capability** | Platform must not reintroduce PRODUCT exclusions as platform “features.” |

Violation of any pair is a constitutional defect, not an engineering preference.

# 11. Engineering Laws

These laws govern **every future software component** under AI-TOS Platform Architecture.

## EL-1 — Constitutional citation

Every provisional module, future application composition, and future Platform document must cite AI-TOS-000 and the applicable higher frozen constitutions.

## EL-2 — Upward-only meaning

Software may depend on higher meaning; it may never redefine higher meaning.

## EL-3 — Dependency direction

```
UAIA → ATI → DDD → PRODUCT → Platform Architecture → Future application compositions → Implementation
```

Dependencies may point **upward** for conformance.  
Dependencies must not create meaning ownership **downward**.

## EL-4 — Capability realization, not capability invention

Future application compositions realize PRODUCT-002 capabilities. They do not invent peer capabilities.

## EL-5 — Semantic ownership follows DDD

Business concept ownership follows DDD Bounded Contexts. Platform packaging cannot reassign ownership.

## EL-6 — Cognitive separations preserved

UAIA/ATI Must-Never-Overlap rules remain binding in all provisional module designs.

## EL-7 — One job per architectural unit

Each Platform-defined architectural unit has one primary constitutional job. Dual ownership of Product + Platform, or Cognition + Host, is forbidden.

## EL-8 — Conformance over convenience

If a convenient structure conflicts with a frozen constitution, the structure is rejected.

## EL-9 — Reviews are audits

Future Platform work is reviewed as conformance to SA-000 and higher layers (ARCH-### pattern), not freestyle redesign.

## EL-10 — What every Platform artifact must conform to

Every Platform artifact must conform to, in order:

Examples include: modules, services, workflows, runtimes, adapters, and future architectural units — without redefining any future document.

1. **AI-TOS-000** Constitution Index  
2. **UAIA v1.0**  
3. **ATI Constitution** (+ applicable ATI-###)  
4. **DDD Constitution** (DDD-000…DDD-010 as applicable)  
5. **PRODUCT Constitution** (PRODUCT-002; PRODUCT-001 as recorded review)  
6. **SA-000** and subsequent frozen Platform documents  
7. Applicable future application-composition Platform laws (future SA-###)

## EL-11 — What must never be allowed inside Platform Architecture

Platform Architecture must never contain:

1. Redefinition of UAIA, ATI, DDD, or PRODUCT;  
2. Product identity claims for the Platform itself;  
3. Ownership claims over Intelligence, Trading cognition, or Business semantics;  
4. Invention of Signal / Strategy / Presentation / Opportunity / Trading-OS as peer product capabilities;  
5. Collapse of Must-Never-Overlap pairs;  
6. Promotion of optional agency to product definition;  
7. Implementation technology as constitutional content;  
8. Substitution of provisional modules for Bounded Contexts or Product Capabilities;  
9. Lateral “platform features” that amend higher constitutions;  
10. Any rule that forces a higher constitution to change meaning without its major version.
