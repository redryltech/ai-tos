# UAIA v1.0

# Universal Autonomous Intelligence Architecture

**Status: FROZEN**  
**Version:** 1.0  
**Document type:** Constitutional architecture specification  
**Scope:** Universal cognitive architecture for autonomous intelligence systems  
**Non-scope:** Software implementation, product specialization, domain-driven design, trading systems  

This document is the immutable constitutional foundation for every future autonomous intelligence system that specializes UAIA, including but not limited to Autonomous Trading Intelligence, Healthcare Intelligence, Robotics Intelligence, Industrial Intelligence, Scientific Intelligence, and Cyber Intelligence.

No specialization may redefine UAIA.  
If a specialization conflicts with UAIA, **UAIA always wins**.

---

# Part 1 — Vision

## 1.1 Purpose

UAIA defines the universal cognitive architecture of an autonomous intelligence: how it senses reality, forms evidence, maintains present understanding, decides, enacts, experiences consequences, learns by proposal, and activates competence only under governance.

UAIA exists so that every specialized intelligence shares one constitutional mind-structure, while remaining free to specialize content, sensors, effectors, and mandates without rewriting the nature of intelligence itself.

## 1.2 Philosophy

1. **Intelligence is a loop, not a library.** Autonomous intelligence continuously couples to reality through Observation, belief, choice, enactment, experience, and governed improvement.
2. **Understanding is the primary architectural asset.** The living mind is the present evidence-bound belief-state, not a static knowledge dump and not a stream of actions.
3. **Descriptive and normative remain separate.** What is believed is not what is permitted or what is chosen.
4. **Evidence precedes belief; belief precedes choice; choice precedes enactment; consequence precedes experience; experience precedes learning; learning precedes activation.**
5. **Production never self-modifies.** Learning proposes; Governance activates.
6. **Abstention is competence.** Doing nothing can be a Decision.
7. **Epistemic honesty is mandatory.** Confidence, unknowns, freshness, invalidation, and consistency are not optional decorations.
8. **Specialization is content, not constitution.** Domains change; UAIA primitives do not.

## 1.3 Why UAIA Exists

Without a frozen universal architecture:

- every product reinvents cognition under different names;
- evidence, belief, choice, and action collapse into each other;
- learning silently rewrites behavior;
- specialization becomes incompatible across domains;
- long-horizon research cannot accumulate on a shared foundation.

UAIA exists to prevent those failures for decades.

## 1.4 Design Principles

1. **One responsibility per primitive**
2. **Explicit interfaces between primitives**
3. **No hidden coupling**
4. **Traceability of belief to Facts**
5. **Traceability of Decision to Understanding and Decision Frame**
6. **Single parent Decision for every Intent**
7. **Concurrency of Execution and Outcome Stream**
8. **Experience as evaluated episode, not raw history**
9. **Candidate Improvement as the only normal path from Learning to activation**
10. **Control owns Attention allocation and permission gating**
11. **Goals are not auto-rewritten by Learning**
12. **Truth (external reality) is never owned by the system**

## 1.5 Non-Goals

UAIA does not define:

- software services, APIs, databases, or deployment topology;
- machine-learning training methods or model families;
- product UX;
- domain ontologies of any specialization (those specialize UAIA);
- organizational charts;
- legal regimes of particular industries.

UAIA defines **cognitive constitution only**.

# Part 2 — Core Architectural Principles

## P-01 Understanding Primacy

- **Why:** The product of intelligence for autonomy and explanation is present situational belief, not mere action.
- **Problem solved:** Systems that are only controllers or only chat interfaces without a living belief-state.
- **Invariant:** Understanding is the primary architectural asset.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Knowledge-first centers; Decision-first centers; Brain-as-monolith metaphors.

## P-02 Stratified Understanding

- **Why:** External environment and agent self-state are different epistemic kinds.
- **Problem solved:** Mixing world opinion with book/body truth.
- **Invariant:** Understanding is stratified (at minimum Environment Understanding and Agent Situation in specializations that require both).
- **Rejected alternatives:** Single fused “total reality understanding.”

## P-03 Understanding as Present Belief-State

- **Why:** “Now” must be explicit, evidence-bound, and falsifiable.
- **Problem solved:** Treating archives or narratives as understanding.
- **Invariant:** Understanding is a present evidence-bound belief-state with epistemic wrappers.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Understanding as knowledge graph identity; Understanding as continuous chatter.

## P-04 Evidence Integrity

- **Why:** Bad evidence destroys all downstream cognition.
- **Problem solved:** Blindness mistaken for calm; claims mistaken for world-state.
- **Invariant:** Observation becomes Fact only through an Acceptance Boundary under integrity rules.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Every percept is a Fact; Fact equals Truth.

## P-05 Cognitive Direction

- **Why:** Scarce cognition must be aimed.
- **Problem solved:** Equal processing of infinite streams.
- **Invariant:** Control includes Attention allocation and permission gating.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Focus as a separate peer primitive; purely emergent attention without Control.

## P-06 Descriptive / Normative Separation

- **Why:** “Is” must not silently become “ought.”
- **Problem solved:** Policy stuffed into belief; belief stuffed into mandate.
- **Invariant:** Goals and Decision Frame carry norms; Understanding carries beliefs.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Constraint Understanding as a belief stratum.

## P-07 Decision Frame Bridge

- **Why:** Belief alone does not entail action.
- **Problem solved:** Jumping from Understanding to Decision without eligibility.
- **Invariant:** Decision Frame binds Goals and Control against Understanding before Decision.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Decision Context as vague sludge; norms inside Understanding.

## P-08 Decision as Selection Including Abstain

- **Why:** Autonomy includes refusal.
- **Problem solved:** Forced action architectures.
- **Invariant:** Decision is normative selection under Frame and Understanding, including abstain.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Signals as Decisions; orders as Decisions.

## P-09 Intent as Enactment Envelope

- **Why:** Choice and enactable directive differ.
- **Problem solved:** Decision leaking effector detail; Action without success conditions.
- **Invariant:** Intent derives from exactly one parent Decision; Action attempts to satisfy Intent.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Intent as order; Intent without Decision.

## P-10 Execution ∥ Outcome Stream

- **Why:** Consequences begin during enactment, not only after completion.
- **Problem solved:** Terminal-only Outcome fallacy.
- **Invariant:** Execution and Outcome Stream are concurrent; Outcome is a stream of situational changes.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Strict Execution-then-Outcome sequence only.

## P-11 Experience as Evaluated Episode

- **Why:** Raw consequences are not learning fuel.
- **Problem solved:** Log-as-learning; PnL-as-experience.
- **Invariant:** Experience is a quality-gated, attributable, evaluated episode.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Every Outcome is Experience; Experience equals Memory.

## P-12 Learning Proposes; Governance Activates

- **Why:** Autonomous improvement without uncontrolled self-modification.
- **Problem solved:** Silent production mutation.
- **Invariant:** Learning emits Candidate Improvements; Governance alone activates competence; production never self-modifies.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Learning equals training that hot-patches behavior; Learning edits Memory live.

## P-13 Memory as Substrate

- **Why:** Retention is not belief-now and not learning-process.
- **Problem solved:** Collapsing Knowledge, Experience, and Understanding into one store-named mind.
- **Invariant:** Memory stores; Understanding is present; Knowledge is durable content in Memory; Learning does not freely rewrite live competence.

## P-14 Specialization Conformance

- **Why:** Long-term multi-domain research requires one constitution.
- **Problem solved:** Each product redefining cognition.
- **Invariant:** Specializations may only specialize content and interfaces; they may not redefine UAIA primitives or laws.
- **Rejected alternatives:** Product-local reinvention of Observation/Fact/Decision/Learning semantics.

# Part 3 — Universal Cognitive Architecture

## 3.1 Primary Flow

```
Reality
  ↓
Observation
  ↓
Facts
  ↓
Assimilation
  ↓
Understanding
  ↓
Decision Frame
  ↓
Reasoning
  ↓
Decision
  ↓
Intent
  ↓
Action
  ↓
Execution  ∥  Outcome Stream
  ↓
Experience
  ↓
Learning
  ↓
Candidate Improvement
  ↓
Governance
  ↓
Activated Competence
```

## 3.2 Cross-Cutting Primitives

### Goals

Standing objectives, mandate aims, horizons, and normative success criteria.  
Goals inform Decision Frame assembly and evaluation of Experience. Goals are not Understanding and are not auto-rewritten by Learning into force.

### Memory

Retention and retrieval substrate for Experiences, standing Knowledge, Policies, Calibration, Candidate Improvement records, and other durable traces. Memory is not Understanding.

### Control

Owns **Attention allocation** and **permission gating**.  
Control aims Observation, Memory retrieval, and Reasoning effort; gates Decision commitment, Action permission, Learning activation path with Governance, and emergency interventions.

### Attention

Not a peer store. Attention is the Control function that continuously allocates scarce cognitive resources toward what presently most matters relative to Goals and Understanding (including the power to starve noise).

## 3.3 How Cross-Cutting Primitives Participate

| Primitive | Participation |
|---|---|
| Goals | Shape Decision Frame; evaluate Experience; constrain Learning activation targets |
| Memory | Supplies analogs and standing competence to Reasoning/Understanding; stores Experiences and Candidates; holds Activated Competence |
| Control | Attention + permissions across the loop; kill/arm; blocks Action on integrity failure |
| Attention | Selects Observation density, retrieval, Reasoning priority |

## 3.4 Epistemic Path vs Normative Path vs Enactment Path vs Evolution Path

- **Epistemic:** Reality → Observation → Facts → Assimilation → Understanding  
- **Normative bind:** Goals + Control + Understanding → Decision Frame  
- **Choice:** Reasoning → Decision → Intent  
- **Enactment:** Action → Execution ∥ Outcome Stream  
- **Evolution:** Experience → Learning → Candidate Improvement → Governance → Activated Competence

# Part 4 — Primitive Definitions

For each primitive: Definition, Purpose, Responsibilities, Inputs, Outputs, Relationships, Lifecycle, Properties, Architecture Invariants, Must Never Become, Must Never Contain, Common Mistakes, Examples, Non-examples.

---

## 4.1 Goals

**Definition:** Standing objectives and mandate criteria that define what success means for the autonomous intelligence.

**Purpose:** Provide normative aim without which Decision is arbitrary.

**Responsibilities:** Declare objectives, horizons, mandate scope, and standing success/failure criteria for evaluation.

**Inputs:** Authority mandate signals (via Observation of Authority realm); Governance-approved mandate changes.

**Outputs:** Active objectives consumed by Decision Frame and Experience evaluation.

**Relationships:** Feeds Decision Frame; constrains Learning activation targets; evaluates Experience valence vs mandate.

**Lifecycle:** Established → Active → Amended (via Authority/Governance) → Retired.

**Properties:** Objective statements; horizon; scope; amendment authority; status.

**Architecture Invariants:** Goals are not beliefs about the world; Learning does not auto-activate Goal rewrites.

**Must Never Become:** Understanding; Decision; Policy sludge without authority.

**Must Never Contain:** Raw Observations; Fact streams; Execution reports.

**Common Mistakes:** Treating Goals as Environment Understanding; letting Learning silently change Goals.

**Examples:** “Preserve safety bounds”; “Prefer abstain under high unknown-set”; domain mandate aims in a specialization.

**Non-examples:** “Price went up”; “I feel bullish”; a single trade idea.

---

## 4.2 Observation

**Definition:** The Attention-aimed sensing interface that samples Reality into pre-commitment percepts tagged by realm (World, Self, Authority, Clock, Channel).

**Purpose:** Couple the intelligence to Reality without yet forming belief.

**Responsibilities:** Produce percepts with time, source, realm, payload, scope, integrity hints.

**Inputs:** Reality; Attention aiming from Control.

**Outputs:** Percepts to the Acceptance Boundary.

**Relationships:** Upstream of Facts; never directly writes Understanding.

**Lifecycle:** Aimed → Sensed → Emitted percept → (Accepted as Fact or Rejected at boundary).

**Properties:** Observe-time; phenomenon-time; source; realm; payload; scope; integrity hints.

**Architecture Invariants:** Observation is not Fact; Observation is not Understanding; Channel realm is first-class.

**Must Never Become:** Belief; Decision; Signal; Indicator.

**Must Never Contain:** Predictions; Decisions; derived indicators presented as senses.

**Common Mistakes:** Treating ticks as Understanding; ignoring Channel integrity; equal Observation of everything.

**Examples:** World stream sample; Self fill acknowledgment percept; Authority halt command percept; Clock session phase; Channel gap percept.

**Non-examples:** “Regime is trending”; a BUY Decision; an indicator value invented internally.

---

## 4.3 Facts

**Definition:** Scoped, time-stamped, provenance-backed evidentiary commitments created when Observation crosses the Acceptance Boundary under Evidence Integrity; usable evidence, not Reality itself.

**Purpose:** Establish admissible evidence for Assimilation and citation.

**Responsibilities:** Represent accepted commitments; support supersession, expiry for live use, retraction, conflict surfacing, revision lineage.

**Inputs:** Observations evaluated at Acceptance Boundary.

**Outputs:** Accepted/provisional/contested Facts for Assimilation and citation.

**Relationships:** Downstream of Observation; upstream of Assimilation; cited by Understanding and Experience evaluation.

**Lifecycle:** Observed → Evaluated → Rejected | Provisional | Accepted | Contested → Used → Superseded | Expired (live use) | Retracted → Historical lineage retained.

**Properties:** Scoped proposition; as-of; acceptance-time; provenance; integrity-status; scope; validity; revision-lineage; commitment confidence.

**Architecture Invariants:** Fact ≠ Truth; claim-Facts ≠ world-state Facts; conflict allowed; silent mutation forbidden.

**Must Never Become:** Truth ownership; Belief; Decision; Reasoning conclusion.

**Must Never Contain:** Unaccepted percepts; Decision rationale as evidence of world.

**Common Mistakes:** News claim as world Fact; Fact equals Truth; Reasoning minting Facts.

**Examples:** “Source A published claim C at T”; “Self fill quantity Q at T”; “Channel gap spanning interval.”

**Non-examples:** “Therefore we should act”; “I understand the regime.”

---

## 4.4 Assimilation

**Definition:** The cognitive process that binds accepted Facts into Understanding by revising beliefs and epistemic wrappers without creating Facts or Decisions.

**Purpose:** Bridge evidence to living belief.

**Responsibilities:** Relevance filtering; stratum routing; reinforce/weaken/create/invalidate/refine beliefs; increase/resolve uncertainty; no-op; defer; preserve traceability.

**Inputs:** Facts; Attention/scope; current Understanding.

**Outputs:** Revised Understanding (or no-op/defer); advanced Understanding revision identity.

**Relationships:** Between Facts and Understanding; may invoke Reasoning for hard binds without merging identities.

**Lifecycle:** Fact arrival → Relevance → Bind/Defer/No-op → Belief operators → Wrapper update → Revision advance.

**Properties:** Operator applied; evidence links; deferral records; consistency accounting.

**Architecture Invariants:** Assimilation never erases Facts; never mints Facts; never mints Decisions; claim scope discipline preserved.

**Must Never Become:** Acceptance Boundary; Decision; Learning.

**Must Never Contain:** Authority to un-accept Facts as if Understanding owned Truth Layer.

**Common Mistakes:** Auto-copy Fact to belief; Understanding revoking Facts; requiring every Fact to change belief.

**Examples:** Reinforce participation belief from breadth Facts; defer claim-Fact from becoming world belief.

**Non-examples:** Placing an order; accepting a percept as Fact.

---

## 4.5 Understanding

**Definition:** The present, stratified, evidence-bound belief-state about the relevant situation, bearing freshness, confidence, unknowns, consistency, and invalidation; the primary architectural asset.

**Purpose:** Provide the living situational truth the intelligence uses as the primary basis for governed choice.

**Responsibilities:** Hold Environment Understanding and Agent Situation strata (as required by specialization); maintain epistemic wrappers; expose revision identity for Decision citation.

**Inputs:** Assimilation updates; Memory retrieval supporting belief formation (not replacing evidence discipline).

**Outputs:** Present belief-state referenced by Decision Frame, Reasoning, Decision, Experience context.

**Relationships:** Downstream of Assimilation; upstream of Decision Frame assembly and Decision; distinct from Memory and Knowledge.

**Lifecycle:** Continuously revised present state with revision identities; beliefs may be invalidated, superseded, expired from live state, or collapsed to unknowns.

**Properties:** Content beliefs by stratum; as-of/freshness; confidence; unknown set; invalidation conditions; consistency status; scope; revision identity.

**Architecture Invariants:** Present belief-state; stratified; evidence-bound; not Knowledge; not Decision; not Opportunity-as-ought.

**Must Never Become:** Knowledge archive identity; Decision; Goals; Financial Reality monolith.

**Must Never Contain:** Standing risk policy law; Pending Intent; raw Observation streams as beliefs.

**Common Mistakes:** Equating price rise Observation with Understanding; omitting epistemic wrappers; mixing strata.

**Examples:** “Regime belief R with confidence c and invalidation I”; “Inventory belief with effector discrepancy unknown.”

**Non-examples:** A strategy document; a Candidate Improvement; an order.

---

## 4.6 Decision Frame

**Definition:** The present normative binding of Goals and Control against Understanding, stating objectives, permissions, standing constraints, headroom readings, eligibility, and frame expiry.

**Purpose:** Bridge descriptive Understanding to normative Decision without stuffing ought into belief.

**Responsibilities:** Assemble Active Objectives, Horizon & Scope, Permission State, Standing Constraints, Eligibility, Headroom Readings, Frame Expiry.

**Inputs:** Goals; Control permission/kill state; Understanding (including Agent Situation headroom readings).

**Outputs:** Frame revision consumed by Reasoning and Decision.

**Relationships:** After Understanding; before Decision; does not modify Understanding.

**Lifecycle:** Assembled → Active → Expired/Rebuilt on Understanding revision, mandate change, kill, clock.

**Properties:** F1–F7 conceptual contents; revision identity; expiry.

**Architecture Invariants:** Frame is not Understanding; Frame is not Decision; Eligibility is the bridge product.

**Must Never Become:** A third Understanding stratum; Constraint Understanding.

**Must Never Contain:** Chosen alternative; Outcome Stream.

**Common Mistakes:** Jumping Understanding → Decision; stale Frame use; putting policy inside Environment Understanding.

**Examples:** “New risk ineligible; reduce-only; horizon H; armed live.”

**Non-examples:** “Market is fragile” as a Frame-native belief (that belongs in Understanding; Frame uses it for eligibility).

---

## 4.7 Reasoning

**Definition:** The process that transforms Understanding, Memory, and Decision Frame into comparisons, predictions, explanations, and candidate alternatives for Decision; includes prediction and metacognitive reflection as modes.

**Purpose:** Deliberate without yet choosing.

**Responsibilities:** Explore alternatives; score/compare; predict consequences; support Assimilation hard binds; support Experience evaluation assistance without owning Experience.

**Inputs:** Understanding; Decision Frame; Memory retrieval; Attention budget from Control.

**Outputs:** Candidate alternatives and analyses to Decision; optional assistive products to Assimilation/Experience evaluation.

**Relationships:** Between Frame and Decision in the choice path; does not mint Facts; does not replace Decision.

**Lifecycle:** Triggered → Deliberating → Emitted alternatives/analysis → Complete (Attention may preempt).

**Properties:** Trigger; scope; alternatives considered; uncertainty about options.

**Architecture Invariants:** Reasoning ≠ Decision; Reasoning ≠ Fact creation; Attention-bounded.

**Must Never Become:** Continuous unbounded consciousness; Fact author; live Policy editor.

**Must Never Contain:** Silent production Learning activation.

**Common Mistakes:** Equating Reasoning with Decision; Reasoning writing Observations; endless deliberation without Decision.

**Examples:** Compare abstain vs reduce vs hedge under Frame eligibility.

**Non-examples:** A committed Decision; an accepted Fact.

---

## 4.8 Decision

**Definition:** The intelligence’s selection of an eligible alternative—including abstain—under the active Decision Frame and Understanding, time-bounded, confidence-bearing, authority-stamped, and fully traceable.

**Purpose:** Resolve what to do or not do now.

**Responsibilities:** Select alternative; stamp status lifecycle; cite Understanding and Frame revisions; authorize Intent formation when Committed.

**Inputs:** Understanding revision; Decision Frame revision; Reasoning alternatives; Control authority.

**Outputs:** Selected/Committed Decision; optional Intent authorization; lifecycle terminals.

**Relationships:** Downstream of Reasoning/Frame; upstream of Intent; cites Understanding.

**Lifecycle:** Selected → Committed → Enacted → Closed; terminals: Expired | Cancelled | Invalidated | Superseded | Rejected (by Control). Candidates remain in Reasoning, not Decision.

**Properties:** Chosen alternative; scope; status; decision time; expiry; decision confidence; Understanding ref; Frame ref; eligibility satisfaction; authority stamp; rationale citations; lineage.

**Architecture Invariants:** Includes abstain; not a signal; not an Intent; not an Action; no silent mutation (use supersession).

**Must Never Become:** Order; broker instruction; indicator.

**Must Never Contain:** Effector payload details belonging to Action specialization.

**Common Mistakes:** Signal as Decision; Action before Decision; no abstain; Decision confidence = Understanding confidence.

**Examples:** “Abstain until unknowns shrink”; “Reduce exposure in scope S.”

**Non-examples:** A tick; a Candidate Improvement; a fill Execution.

---

## 4.9 Intent

**Definition:** The actionable commitment envelope derived from a Committed Decision: scoped, constrained, time-bounded directive stating what successful enactment means and what Action is authorized to attempt.

**Purpose:** Contract between Decision and Action.

**Responsibilities:** Carry enactment objective, constraints, success/cancel conditions, expiry, authority; lifecycle including pause/resume where applicable.

**Inputs:** Parent Decision; Frame constraints still in force; optional tactical refinement from Reasoning in service of that Decision.

**Outputs:** Authorized directive for Action; status transitions; closure signals.

**Relationships:** Exactly one parent Decision; one Decision may create many Intents; upstream of Action.

**Lifecycle:** Created → Authorized → (Ready) → Active ↔ Paused → Completed | Abandoned; also Cancelled | Expired | Superseded.

**Properties:** Parent Decision; enactment objective; scope; constraints; success condition; cancel/invalidate conditions; horizon/expiry; authority stamp; status; priority when contention exists; lineage.

**Architecture Invariants:** Not Decision; not Action; not Execution; single parent Decision; supersession may keep Decision fixed if alternative unchanged.

**Must Never Become:** Order identity; multi-parent Intent; Decision substitute.

**Must Never Contain:** Full strategy programs as identity; broker protocol fields as ontology.

**Common Mistakes:** Intent without Decision; merging Intent and Action; “Executed” as Intent state.

**Examples:** “Achieve reduce-to-threshold T in scope S before expiry E under constraints C.”

**Non-examples:** The Decision “reduce risk”; a motor PWM command as ontology.

---

## 4.10 Action

**Definition:** The attempt to affect Reality/effectors in accordance with an Intent’s constraints and success conditions.

**Purpose:** Enact.

**Responsibilities:** Initiate and manage attempts; respect Intent constraints; stop on Intent terminal states; emit attempts into Execution.

**Inputs:** Active/authorized Intent; Control permission.

**Outputs:** Action attempts; linkage to Execution progress.

**Relationships:** Subordinate to Intent; produces Execution; contributes to Outcome Stream indirectly through Reality coupling.

**Lifecycle:** Armed → Attempting → Succeeded attempt path | Failed attempt path | Stopped (Intent cancelled/expired/superseded).

**Properties:** Parent Intent; attempt identity; time; status.

**Architecture Invariants:** No Action without Intent in the normal path; Action ≠ Decision; Action ≠ Outcome.

**Must Never Become:** Experience; Learning; Fact minting by assertion.

**Must Never Contain:** Redesign of Goals.

**Common Mistakes:** Action before Decision/Intent; continuing Action on dead Intent.

**Examples:** Attempt effector change to satisfy Intent.

**Non-examples:** Understanding revision; Candidate Improvement.

---

## 4.11 Execution

**Definition:** Effector-side progress and terminal reports of Action attempts.

**Purpose:** Represent how attempts are going in the effector world.

**Responsibilities:** Track progress, success/failure/reject/partial effector results.

**Inputs:** Action attempts; effector acknowledgments (via Observation ultimately for known Execution).

**Outputs:** Execution status/progress consumed for control of attempts and for recognizing Outcomes.

**Relationships:** Concurrent with Outcome Stream; not identical to Outcome.

**Lifecycle:** Started → In progress → Completed | Failed | Cancelled | Partial terminal as applicable.

**Properties:** Attempt refs; effector status; times; completion flags.

**Architecture Invariants:** Execution ≠ Outcome; success Execution ≠ Goal-positive Outcome.

**Must Never Become:** Experience; Understanding rewrite bypassing Assimilation.

**Must Never Contain:** Evaluative lesson extraction as identity.

**Common Mistakes:** Fill/ack as Outcome; “Executed” on Decision/Intent as synonyms.

**Examples:** Effector acknowledges progress; attempt rejected.

**Non-examples:** “Mission objective achieved” without situational confirmation.

---

## 4.12 Outcome (Outcome Stream)

**Definition:** Scoped, evidence-bound records of consequential change in Agent Situation and/or Environment associated with an enactment path; realized as a **stream** that may begin during Execution, run concurrently, and continue after Execution ends.

**Purpose:** Represent situational consequences, intended and unintended.

**Responsibilities:** Record situational deltas; attribution to Intent/Action with confidence; completeness; revision when later Facts correct.

**Inputs:** Observation → Facts of consequential change; attribution binding to enactment path.

**Outputs:** Outcome stream elements for Experience formation.

**Relationships:** Concurrent with Execution; upstream of Experience; never equals Execution.

**Lifecycle (cognitive):** Detected → Evidenced → Attributed → Closed (episode window) → Revised as needed.

**Properties:** Scoped change; time; Fact provenance; attribution; completeness; confidence; evaluative tags vs Intent/Goals optional.

**Architecture Invariants:** Stream not only terminal event; known Outcomes require Observation; failed Actions still have Outcomes; Experience consumes closed attributed stream windows.

**Must Never Become:** Execution; Experience; Learning.

**Must Never Contain:** Silent unobserved omniscience claims.

**Common Mistakes:** Terminal-only Outcome; ignoring unintended Outcomes; Outcomes without Observation.

**Examples:** During attempt, nearby state shifts; after completion, lagged situational effect.

**Non-examples:** “Motor on” as mission Outcome; raw Execution ack as Outcome.

---

## 4.13 Experience

**Definition:** A scoped, time-bounded, attributable, evaluated episode binding context and (when applicable) Decision/Intent/Action to an Outcome stream, with evaluative labels of sufficient quality to serve as learning fuel.

**Purpose:** Provide governed Learning with evaluated episodes rather than raw consequences.

**Responsibilities:** Open/grow/close episode windows; attribute; evaluate; grade evaluability; revise on new evidence; discard low quality.

**Inputs:** Outcome streams; Understanding/Frame refs; Decision/Intent refs; optional Reasoning assistance.

**Outputs:** Evaluated Experiences (or Discarded); inputs to Learning.

**Relationships:** Downstream of Outcome Stream; upstream of Learning; stored in Memory; not Memory itself.

**Lifecycle:** Candidate → Open → Growing → Closed → Evaluated → Archived; also Discarded; Revised.

**Properties:** Episode identity; scope/horizon; status; context refs; enactment refs; Outcome refs; attribution; evaluation record; quality grade; time bounds; revision lineage.

**Architecture Invariants:** Not every Outcome becomes Experience; Experience can be wrong; can exist without Learning; long horizons allowed; weak attribution ⇒ weak/non-Experience.

**Must Never Become:** Log file; PnL identity; Memory; Learning; Knowledge.

**Must Never Contain:** Automatic production Policy edits.

**Common Mistakes:** Every Outcome = Experience; Experience = history; instant Experience at first ack; no attribution.

**Examples:** Evaluated reduce-risk episode with prediction error and attribution confidence.

**Non-examples:** Unlabeled Outcome stream; a Candidate Improvement.

---

## 4.14 Learning

**Definition:** The process that transforms admissible Experiences (and other gated evidence sources) into Candidate Improvements—or explicit no-ops/refusals—without activating live competence.

**Purpose:** Improve future competence by proposal only.

**Responsibilities:** Gate inputs; reject/no-op; generalize carefully; emit Candidates including deprecation/uncertainty proposals; never activate.

**Inputs:** Experiences; research; simulation/counterfactuals; human teaching—all gated.

**Outputs:** Candidate Improvements; no-op/reject records.

**Relationships:** Downstream of Experience; upstream of Candidate Improvement; does not replace Governance.

**Lifecycle (Learning act):** Triggered → Considered → Emit | No-op | Reject → Recorded.

**Properties:** Trigger; sources considered; emission/no-op record.

**Architecture Invariants:** Learning never modifies production directly; may learn wrongly hence must not activate; may refuse to learn; online/offline are modes not identity.

**Must Never Become:** Training-as-hot-patch; Memory identity; Reasoning identity; Governance.

**Must Never Contain:** Silent Goal/safety/kill-law rewrites into force.

**Common Mistakes:** Learning = fine-tuning production; every Experience forces Learning; skipping Candidates.

**Examples:** Emit Candidate Policy narrowing after many graded Experiences.

**Non-examples:** Live Policy flip; Assimilation belief update.

---

## 4.15 Candidate Improvement

**Definition:** A proposed competence change emitted by Learning, carrying evidence, scope, generalization confidence, reversibility, activation risk, and explainability, awaiting Governance.

**Purpose:** Sole normal bridge from Learning to activation.

**Responsibilities:** Represent proposed change and target (Knowledge/Policy/Calibration/Deprecation/Meta-learning); carry review status.

**Inputs:** Learning emissions.

**Outputs:** Submissions to Governance; archival after decision.

**Relationships:** Between Learning and Governance; upon approval becomes Activated Competence path.

**Lifecycle:** Candidate → Validating/Under Review → Approved → Activated → Archived; or Rejected → Archived; Activated may Roll Back.

**Properties:** Proposed change; target; evidence support; scope; generalization confidence; reversibility; activation risk; explainability; status.

**Architecture Invariants:** No Candidate ⇒ no normal activation; not yet Knowledge; not Experience.

**Must Never Become:** Live Policy by emission alone; Fact.

**Must Never Contain:** Activation without Governance.

**Common Mistakes:** Treating Candidate as activated Knowledge; anecdote as general Candidate without scope/confidence.

**Examples:** “Deprecate rule R in regime Q”; “Calibrate confidence map M.”

**Non-examples:** An Experience; an Understanding revision.

---

## 4.16 Governance

**Definition:** The authority process that validates, approves, rejects, activates, monitors, and rolls back Candidate Improvements into or out of live competence.

**Purpose:** Ensure production never self-modifies.

**Responsibilities:** Judge Candidates; enforce non-auto-learn zones; activate/reject/rollback; record authority.

**Inputs:** Candidate Improvements; validation evidence; Authority policy for approval.

**Outputs:** Activation/rejection/rollback decisions; Activated Competence updates.

**Relationships:** Downstream of Candidate Improvement; writes Activated Competence into Memory-resident standing content; collaborates with Control gates.

**Lifecycle:** Review → Approve | Reject → Activate → Monitor → Archive | Rollback.

**Properties:** Authority identity; decision; rationale; time; linkage to Candidate.

**Architecture Invariants:** Governance alone activates competence in the normal path; Governance does not invent evidence; safety/Goals hard laws are non-auto-learn into force.

**Must Never Become:** Learning; Memory substrate identity; Understanding.

**Must Never Contain:** Silent activation without Candidate.

**Common Mistakes:** Equating statistical validation with Governance authority; bypassing Governance for “small” updates.

**Examples:** Approve Candidate after review; roll back activated Candidate after harm.

**Non-examples:** Assimilation; Decision to abstain in the live loop.

---

## 4.17 Memory

**Definition:** The retention and retrieval substrate for durable and episodic content, including Experiences, standing Knowledge, Policies, Calibration, Candidate records, and related traces.

**Purpose:** Remember without being the present mind.

**Responsibilities:** Store and retrieve; support Reasoning and Learning; hold Activated Competence content.

**Inputs:** Experiences; Activated Competence; Candidate archives; other durable traces.

**Outputs:** Retrieved content to Reasoning/Understanding support/Learning.

**Relationships:** Distinct from Understanding; contains Knowledge as content kind; stores Experiences.

**Lifecycle:** Write → Retain → Retrieve → Deprecate/Archive (via governed paths where required).

**Properties:** Content type; identifiers; retrieval keys; retention status.

**Architecture Invariants:** Memory ≠ Understanding; Memory ≠ Learning; Knowledge lives in Memory.

**Must Never Become:** Present belief-state; Decision Frame.

**Must Never Contain:** Claiming to be Truth.

**Common Mistakes:** Equating Memory with Experience; equating Memory with Knowledge only.

**Examples:** Retrieve analogous Experiences; store activated Policy.

**Non-examples:** Live Decision; ephemeral Attention aim alone.

---

## 4.18 Control

**Definition:** The cross-cutting primitive owning Attention allocation and permission gating across the cognitive loop.

**Purpose:** Steer scarce cognition and enforce what may proceed.

**Responsibilities:** Allocate Attention; gate Decision commitment, Action, and activation pathways with Governance; emergency intervention; enforce integrity stand-downs.

**Inputs:** Goals; Understanding salience; Channel/integrity signals; Authority kill/arm; Governance activation rules.

**Outputs:** Attention aims; permission allow/deny; kill/halt states.

**Relationships:** Shapes Observation/Reasoning/Memory retrieval intensity; gates Decision/Action; partners with Governance on activation.

**Lifecycle:** Continuous; mode changes (armed/killed/limited) over time.

**Properties:** Permission state; Attention allocation state; kill/arm; intervention records.

**Architecture Invariants:** Control includes Attention; Control ≠ Reasoning; Control ≠ Decision selection identity.

**Must Never Become:** Understanding; Learning activation without Governance for competence updates.

**Must Never Contain:** Belief content as its identity.

**Common Mistakes:** Dropping Control from diagrams; inventing Focus as peer primitive duplicate.

**Examples:** Starve low-salience Observation; block Action on effector discrepancy; halt.

**Non-examples:** Choosing among eligible alternatives (Decision); assimilating a Fact.

---

## 4.19 Attention

**Definition:** The Control function that continuously allocates scarce cognitive resources—Observation density, Memory retrieval, Reasoning effort, Evaluation priority—toward what presently most reduces decision-relevant uncertainty or risk relative to Goals and Understanding.

**Purpose:** Cognitive resource allocation.

**Responsibilities:** Select targets and intensity; starve noise; escalate on salience.

**Inputs:** Goals; Understanding (salience/unknowns/fragility); standing priorities in Memory; Control mode.

**Outputs:** Allocation directives to Observation, Memory retrieval, Reasoning, Experience evaluation priority.

**Relationships:** Owned by Control; not a peer store primitive.

**Lifecycle:** Continuous reallocation.

**Properties:** Targets; intensities; starve lists; time.

**Architecture Invariants:** Attention ⊂ Control; Salience is derived input, not a peer primitive.

**Must Never Become:** Peer primitive rivaling Understanding; Goals replacement.

**Must Never Contain:** Decision choice identity.

**Common Mistakes:** Naming Focus as tenth peer primitive; treating Attention as Observation.

**Examples:** Dense Observation on high-unknown scope; defer Reasoning on noise.

**Non-examples:** A Fact; a Candidate Improvement.

---

## 4.20 Activated Competence

**Definition:** Standing competence content in Memory that has been activated by Governance from approved Candidate Improvements—including Knowledge, Policy, Calibration, and governed Deprecation effects—available to influence future cycles.

**Purpose:** Make improvement real only after authority.

**Responsibilities:** Represent live standing updates post-activation; remain reversible when marked reversible; subject to rollback.

**Inputs:** Governance activation of Candidates.

**Outputs:** Effects on future Reasoning, Decision Frame constraints, procedural behavior, calibration of confidence, etc., without bypassing Observation/Fact discipline.

**Relationships:** Downstream of Governance; resident in Memory; never emitted directly by Learning.

**Lifecycle:** Activated → Monitoring → Stable | Rolled Back → Archived.

**Properties:** Source Candidate ref; activation authority; target type; scope; reversibility; status.

**Architecture Invariants:** Only via Governance; Learning never writes Activated Competence directly; production never self-modifies.

**Must Never Become:** Experience; Fact; Understanding revision by fiat.

**Must Never Contain:** Unapproved Learning emissions.

**Common Mistakes:** Hot-patching competence from a single Experience; irreversible silent activation.

**Examples:** Activated Policy; activated Calibration map; activated Deprecation of a prior rule.

**Non-examples:** Candidate still under review; ephemeral Reasoning alternative.

# Part 6 — Lifecycle Definitions

## 6.1 Facts

`Observed → Evaluated → Rejected | Provisional | Accepted | Contested → Used → Superseded | Expired(live) | Retracted → Historical lineage`

## 6.2 Understanding

Continuous present state with monotonic revision identities for citations; belief operators: reinforce, weaken, create, invalidate, refine, uncertainty↑/↓, no-op, defer; epistemic wrappers always updated honestly.

## 6.3 Decision

`Selected → Committed → Enacted → Closed`  
Terminals: `Expired | Cancelled | Invalidated | Superseded | Rejected(by Control)`  
(Pre-decisional candidates belong to Reasoning.)

## 6.4 Intent

`Created → Authorized → (Ready) → Active ↔ Paused → Completed | Abandoned`  
Also: `Cancelled | Expired | Superseded`

## 6.5 Execution

`Started → In progress → Completed | Failed | Cancelled | Partial-terminal`

## 6.6 Outcome (Stream Elements / Windows)

Element: `Detected → Evidenced → Attributed → Closed → Revised`  
Stream window closes for Experience candidacy; residual effects may continue after Execution completion.

## 6.7 Experience

`Candidate → Open → Growing → Closed → Evaluated → Archived`  
Also: `Discarded | Revised`

## 6.8 Learning (Act)

`Triggered → Considered → Emit Candidate(s) | No-op | Reject inputs → Recorded`

## 6.9 Candidate Improvement

`Candidate → Validating/Under Review → Approved → Activated → Archived`  
Also: `Rejected → Archived`; `Rolled Back`

## 6.10 Governance

`Review → Approve | Reject → Activate → Monitor → Archive | Rollback`

# Part 7 — Architecture Invariants

1. Observation never creates beliefs.  
2. Facts are accepted evidence, not Truth.  
3. Fact ≠ claim-world conflation: claim-Facts and world-state Facts remain distinct.  
4. Understanding is a present evidence-bound belief-state.  
5. Understanding is the primary architectural asset.  
6. Understanding is stratified where Environment and Agent Situation both apply.  
7. Decision Frame never changes Understanding.  
8. Reasoning never invents Facts.  
9. Decision never creates Facts.  
10. Decision must reference an Understanding revision and a Decision Frame revision.  
11. Abstain is a valid Decision.  
12. Intent is not Decision; Action is not Intent; Execution is not Outcome.  
13. Every Intent has exactly one parent Decision.  
14. Execution and Outcome Stream are concurrent.  
15. Outcome is a stream of situational changes, not only a terminal event.  
16. Known Outcomes require Observation → Facts.  
17. Outcome is not Experience; Experience is not Learning.  
18. Not every Outcome becomes Experience; not every Experience causes Learning.  
19. Learning never modifies production directly.  
20. Learning emits Candidate Improvements (or no-ops), not Activated Competence.  
21. Governance alone activates competence in the normal path.  
22. Production never self-modifies.  
23. Knowledge exists inside Memory; Memory ≠ Understanding.  
24. Goals are not auto-rewritten into force by Learning.  
25. Control owns Attention allocation and permission gating.  
26. Attention is not a separate peer store primitive.  
27. Every belief in Understanding must be evidence-traceable or explicitly thin/unsupported.  
28. Silent mutation of Facts, Decisions, Intents, Outcomes, Experiences, or Candidates is forbidden; use revision/supersession lineages.  
29. Specializations may not redefine these invariants.

# Part 9 — Architecture Laws

**Law 1.** No primitive may assume responsibilities owned by another primitive.  

**Law 2.** Every belief in Understanding must trace to accepted Facts or be explicitly marked thin/unsupported.  

**Law 3.** Every Decision must reference an Understanding revision and a Decision Frame revision.  

**Law 4.** Every Intent must have exactly one parent Decision.  

**Law 5.** Every known Outcome must originate through Observation (then Facts) of consequential change.  

**Law 6.** Every normal Learning proposal must be grounded in admissible evidence, primarily Experience, and emitted as Candidate Improvement.  

**Law 7.** Every production competence update requires Governance activation.  

**Law 8.** Each primitive owns exactly one conceptual responsibility.  

**Law 9.** Reality (Truth) is never a system-owned primitive.  

**Law 10.** Specializations must conform to UAIA; UAIA supersedes conflicting specialization claims.  

**Law 11.** Breaking semantic changes require a new UAIA major version.  

**Law 12.** Control may halt enactment; halt is not a substitute for Decision ontology in the normal path, and emergency Control acts remain auditable and distinct from standard Intent authorship.

# Part 11 — Architecture Review Rules

## 11.1 Foundation Rule

UAIA is the foundation. Every autonomous intelligence architecture review begins with UAIA conformance.

## 11.2 Specialization Rule

Specializations (including ATI and all others) may:

- define domain content ontologies under Understanding strata;
- define domain sensors/effectors under Observation/Action;
- define domain mandates under Goals;
- define domain Governance procedures consistent with UAIA Laws;

Specializations may not:

- redefine UAIA primitives;
- collapse Must-Never-Overlap pairs;
- allow Learning to activate production directly;
- replace Decision Frame with Understanding-norm fusion;
- treat Execution as Outcome or Outcome as Experience.

## 11.3 Conflict Rule

If a specialization conflicts with UAIA, **UAIA always wins**.  
The specialization must change.

## 11.4 Review Checklist (Mandatory)

1. Is Understanding still the primary asset?  
2. Are Observation → Facts → Assimilation → Understanding intact?  
3. Is Decision Frame present before Decision?  
4. Is abstain representable as Decision?  
5. Does every Intent have one parent Decision?  
6. Are Execution and Outcome concurrent streams?  
7. Is Experience evaluated and gated?  
8. Does Learning emit Candidates only?  
9. Does Governance alone activate competence?  
10. Are Goals/safety non-auto-learned into force?  
11. Is Control/Attention explicit?  
12. Are all Must-Never-Overlap rules preserved?

## 11.5 Amendment Rule

Proposals that alter UAIA semantics require a new major version initiative (UAIA v2.0), not silent drift inside a specialization.
